# RecChat Source Policy

## Preferred sources

1. Works written or spoken by the historical figure
2. Letters, speeches, interviews, and lectures
3. Authoritative archival collections
4. Government or university repositories
5. Scholarly secondary sources used only for context

## Excluded sources

- Unsourced quotation websites
- Social media posts
- Automatically generated biographies
- Content with unclear attribution
- Low-quality OCR with no reliable original
- Sources with uncertain reuse rights
- Secondary material presented as the figure's own words

## Approval requirements

A source may be ingested only when:

- its author or speaker is clearly identified;
- its origin is recorded;
- its source level is classified;
- its rights status has been reviewed;
- the document is readable;
- page or section references can be preserved.

## Source hierarchy

Primary sources should be preferred for viewpoint questions.

Secondary sources may provide historical context but should not be used to invent first-person beliefs.
